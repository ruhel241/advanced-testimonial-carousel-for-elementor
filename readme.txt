Create beautiful testimonial and review carousels with Elementor. Add custom testimonials or display Google Reviews with multiple business locations, flexible layouts, responsive controls, styling options, and more.

== Description ==

Advanced Testimonial Carousel For Elementor is a powerful and flexible Elementor widget for displaying customer testimonials and reviews in beautiful carousel and slider layouts.

You can create your own custom testimonials or display real reviews from Google. Choose the review source that best fits your website and customize the appearance to match your design.

With the Pro version, you can enable or disable Custom Reviews and Google Reviews independently, giving you complete control over which type of reviews are displayed.

Whether you want to showcase customer testimonials, Google Business reviews, or reviews from multiple business locations, Advanced Testimonial Carousel For Elementor provides flexible options to build an attractive and responsive review section.

== Reviews & Google Reviews ==

=== Custom Reviews ===

Add your own customer testimonials manually and customize the review content, author name, designation, image, rating, and other display options.

With the Pro version, you can enable or disable Custom Reviews whenever you want.

=== Google Reviews ===

Display reviews from your Google Business Profile directly on your website.

To use Google Reviews, you need to provide a Google API Key. After configuring the API key, you can add your Google Business location using its Place ID.

You can also add multiple Place IDs, making it possible to display reviews from multiple businesses or business locations on your website.

For example, if you have multiple branches or business locations, you can add the Place ID for each location and manage them from the plugin.

Google Reviews also include additional Pro features such as review sorting, rating filtering, date sorting, and automatic review fetching.

=== Choose Your Review Source ===

The Pro version allows you to control Custom Reviews and Google Reviews independently.

You can:

* Enable Custom Reviews
* Disable Custom Reviews
* Enable Google Reviews
* Disable Google Reviews
* Use only Custom Reviews
* Use only Google Reviews
* Use both Custom Reviews and Google Reviews

This gives you complete flexibility to decide which reviews should appear on your website.

== Google Reviews ==

The Google Reviews feature allows you to display reviews from your Google Business locations inside your Elementor testimonial carousel.

To use Google Reviews:

1. Add your Google API Key.
2. Add the Place ID of your Google Business location.
3. Select your preferred review settings.
4. Fetch and display your Google Reviews.

You can add multiple Place IDs if you have multiple businesses, branches, offices, or locations.

For example:

* Business Location 1 - Place ID
* Business Location 2 - Place ID
* Business Location 3 - Place ID

This makes it easy to manage reviews from multiple business locations.

Google Reviews features are available in the Pro version.

== Installation ==

This section describes how to install the plugin and get it working.

1. Upload the plugin files to the `/wp-content/plugins/` directory, or install the plugin through the WordPress Plugins screen.
2. Activate the plugin through the Plugins screen.
3. Make sure Elementor is installed and activated.
4. Open Elementor and add the Advanced Testimonial Carousel widget to your page.
5. Configure your testimonials and carousel settings.
6. For Google Reviews, configure the Google API Key and add your Google Place ID from the Pro settings.

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

= Is the plugin responsive? =

Yes. Advanced Testimonial Carousel For Elementor is fully responsive and works across desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.

= Can I add unlimited testimonials? =

Yes. You can add unlimited custom testimonials and display multiple reviews in the carousel.

= Can I use Custom Reviews? =

Yes. You can create and display your own custom testimonials.

= Can I use Google Reviews? =

Yes. Google Reviews are available in the Pro version. You need to configure a Google API Key and add your Google Business Place ID.

= Do I need a Google API Key to use Google Reviews? =

Yes. A Google API Key is required to connect the plugin with Google and retrieve Google Business reviews.

= What is a Google Place ID? =

A Google Place ID identifies a specific business or location on Google. You can use the Place ID of your business location to retrieve its Google Reviews.

= Can I add multiple Google Place IDs? =

Yes. The Pro version supports multiple Place IDs, allowing you to manage reviews from multiple businesses or business locations.

= Can I enable or disable Google Reviews? =

Yes. The Pro version provides an option to enable or disable Google Reviews.

= Can I enable or disable Custom Reviews? =

Yes. The Pro version provides a separate option to enable or disable Custom Reviews.

= Can I use both Custom Reviews and Google Reviews? =

Yes. The Pro version allows you to use Custom Reviews and Google Reviews according to your requirements. You can enable both or use only one of them.

= Can I use only Google Reviews? =

Yes. You can disable Custom Reviews and use only Google Reviews.

= Can I use only Custom Reviews? =

Yes. You can disable Google Reviews and use only Custom Reviews.

= Can I customize the testimonial image? =

Yes. You can customize the image size, shape, border radius, and other image styling options.

= Can I customize the testimonial background? =

Yes. You can customize the background color. Additional gradient and advanced styling options are available in the Pro version.

= Can I customize the testimonial content? =

Yes. You can customize the author name, designation, content, typography, colors, spacing, and other styling options.

= Can I customize the rating? =

Yes. Rating and advanced rating styling options are available in the Pro version.
